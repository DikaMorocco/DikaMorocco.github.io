What We Are

About Dicastal Morocco

Dicastal Morocco was incorporated on June 26th, 2019 in Atlantic Free Zone, Kénitra. We manufacture world-class aluminum alloy wheels and precision automotive components utilizing leading-edge digital casting, machining, automated finishing, and continuous innovation.

01

Our Mission

To become a globally respected great enterprise that constantly creates value for its customers, employees, shareholders and society.

02

Our Vision

To become the world's leading enterprise group with core technologies and self-owned brands for lightweight modularized new-type composite parts and components.

03. Our Values

Our organizational culture and daily operations across all plant divisions are guided by six fundamental corporate commitments:

诚信
Corporate Commitment 01 / 06 诚信

诚信 — Honesty

“As a sincere man could crack even the hardest stones, it is credibility that gives us the world.”

Honesty is the founding principle and business philosophy of CITIC Group. We demand employees guard the honor of the nation, reputation of the enterprise and authority of the law. Be credible, reputable, ethical and disciplined. Keep promise, treat clients and partners with candor, honesty and a keen sense of responsibility.

创新
Corporate Commitment 02 / 06 创新

创新 — Innovation

“To keep up with the times, constant innovation is the key.”

Innovation is an inexhaustible force and advantage for the development of CITIC Group. In the development of its wide range of business, CITIC Group needs to keep an open mind, update its ideas, keep pace with the times, adapt to new situations and challenges, dare to break the convention and be ready to take pioneering roles. The Group should forge ahead, choose innovative actions, be bold to explore and practice, and win market opportunities through innovation and thus leading future development.

凝聚
Corporate Commitment 03 / 06 凝聚

凝聚 — Cohesion

“Nothing is impossible when people make concerted efforts.”

Cohesion is the source of strength and the important embodiment of the overall competitiveness of CITIC Group. CITIC Group actively promotes and fosters the spirit of sincere cooperation among its employees. To firmly establish a holistic perspective, employees are expected to have consistent objectives, make consistent moves, think and act accordingly, and realize optimal allocation and sharing of resources on the basis of solidarity.

融合
Corporate Commitment 04 / 06 融合

融合 — Harmony

“The sea is vast because it takes in water from many rivers.”

Harmony is a characteristic of CITIC Group in its internal management and a strategy of action in its external affairs. It requires that the corporation should set up a global vision, keep an open mind, adhere to the principles of solidarity, tolerance, mutual respect and inclusiveness internally, and uphold a vision of cooperation, harmony, joint development, and mutual benefits externally.

In the face of international and domestic markets, CITIC Group has an open mind and broad vision. It excels at integrating advanced ideas, culture, and technology in the world for its own use. It can accommodate reasonable individual differences and seek common ground while reserving differences. Individuals with different specialties and cultural backgrounds are welcome to work in harmony under the common development goals of CITIC Group and strive to achieve their individual goals. Branches with different development conditions and historical backgrounds are encouraged to resolve contradictions and differences under the guidance of the common values of CITIC Group, and to work together to deliver greater value.

奉献
Corporate Commitment 05 / 06 奉献

奉献 — Dedication

“True dedication means serving the company with diligence, integrity, and excellence.”

Dedication is a core value of CITIC Group's corporate culture. The Group expects its employees to demonstrate commitment to the company and their professional responsibilities, perform their duties with diligence and integrity, and strive for excellence in their work. Employees are encouraged to act ethically, uphold professional standards, fulfill their social responsibilities, contribute positively to society, and demonstrate respect, honesty, and a willingness to support others.

卓越
Corporate Commitment 06 / 06 卓越

卓越 — Excellence

“In a boat race, the one who pushes wins.”

Excellence is the value orientation and goal of CITIC Group. The Group requires its employees not to be mediocre but to be ambitious, to pursue higher goals, better quality, and better benefits in their development. Employees are encouraged to constantly surpass themselves and others, to strive to achieve new leaps and breakthroughs, to be leaders in the development of the industries and be setters of industry standards, and to maintain the vigor of the corporation.

Global Manufacturing Scale

Dicastal Morocco operate world-class facilities in Morocco, delivering high-volume OEM components to international automotive leaders across Europe and North America.

Industry 4.0 & Automation

Our plants integrate intelligent foundry casting, automated machining cells, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), proprietary DMS tracking, and real-time 100% digital X-ray inspection for total traceability.

Quality & Sustainability

Certified under IATF 16949, ISO 14001, ISO 50001, ISO 14064 and ISO 45001. We prioritize low-carbon melting technologies, circular aluminum scrap recycling, energy optimization, and zero-accident occupational safety.
